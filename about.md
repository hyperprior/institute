# Hyperprior Institute

A hyperprior is a prior on priors: beliefs about your beliefs. The Hyperprior
Institute is a lab run by **hyperprior**, its scientist, for chasing interesting
questions in statistics, machine learning and wherever else curiosity leads, and
for building the tools that make the next question cheaper to chase.

Ideas start as **sparks**. They become **sketches** once they run, **labs** once
they stick, and sometimes spin out as **libraries**. The ones that don't make it
get a write-up and a decent burial. Everything here is built in the open,
including the machinery that runs it.
